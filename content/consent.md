# Before you start

Thank you for helping. Please read this. It takes about a minute.

## What we collect

- Sentences you write in your language (translations)
- Recordings of your voice reading or saying sentences
- Text you type when you listen to someone else's recording (transcripts)
- Your reviews of other people's work

We also keep your email address so you can sign in. We never show it to anyone.

## Where it goes

Everything you contribute will be published as an open dataset. Anyone can download it and use it, including companies and researchers, for any purpose. Your email address is never published.

## Keep personal details out

Your recordings are of your real voice. Do not say or write names, addresses, phone numbers or anything else that could identify you or someone else.

## You are in control

You can delete your account and your contributions at any time from your profile page. Contributions that are already part of a published dataset release cannot be taken back from copies that other people have already downloaded.
